Record a problem

Choose Record next middle-click, return to the page and middle-click the troublesome item once. Wait about 10 seconds, then return here to copy the report. If a background copy is still loading, wait for it to finish before copying.

Recording is optional and local. Reports omit typed form values, page titles, URL queries and fragments. Address paths and page relationship IDs may still identify private content: review the report before sharing. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Open the add-on's Preferences in Firefox to record a click.

The last background-copy status will appear here.
